## Problem evidence
- **Poisoning and dealer knowledge.** Down To Earth, 7 March 2018, "Death by pesticides": Maharashtra told the Lok Sabha pesticides killed 272 farmers in four years; 21 farmers died in Yavatmal in 2017; a PAN India study (October 2017) found dealers unaware of, or feigning ignorance of, approved uses and waiting periods, and that packets often lack approved uses, doses and waiting times. https://www.downtoearth.org.in/agriculture/death-by-pesticides-farmers-continue-to-die-as-india-flouts-global-norms-59830
- **Rules change after products reach shops.** Insecticides (Prohibition) Order 2023 (dated 29 Sep 2023) cancelled dicofol, dinocap and methomyl and omitted label claims on listed crops for carbofuran, malathion, quinalphos, mancozeb, oxyfluorfen, dimethoate and chlorpyriphos. https://www.legitquest.com/act/insecticides-prohibition-order-2023/E1BF

## Existing solutions and the gap
- **Plantix** (GSMA case study): photo diagnosis of crop pests and diseases, 20 languages, outbreak alerts. The article does not mention reading pesticide packets, product safety or waiting periods. https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/programme/agritech/detecting-and-managing-crop-pests-and-diseases-with-ai-insights-from-plantix/
- **Official CIB&RC lists** exist as PDFs on the PPQS website (public notice of 14 June 2023 lists them) but are English documents for professionals. https://ppqs.gov.in/sites/default/files/public_notice-updation.pdf
- Gap addressed: product-level, crop-specific, multilingual guidance at the point of use, from the packet itself.

## Toxicity colour code
Red: extremely toxic (oral LD50 1-50 mg/kg); yellow: highly toxic (51-500); blue: moderately toxic (501-5000); green: slightly toxic (above 5000). Governed by the Insecticides Act 1968 and Insecticides Rules 1971. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toxicity_label

## Helplines
